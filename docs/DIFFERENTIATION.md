# Why Cuelira

I built Cuelira for an actor preparing a short scene alone. My intended distinction is narrow: **a deliberately performed facial cue changes the scripted partner's response, then the actor reviews the causal events of that take.**

It is not enough to show expression bars. The important unit is `accepted observation → scene transition → Rowan's answer → timestamped review`. In manual practice or a manual recovery, the unit is explicitly `actor action → manual continuation`.

The competitor research changed the claim. Breathing-responsive rehearsal, expression-controlled games, interactive fictional characters and speech-cued actor readers already exist. I do not claim to have invented any of these categories. Cuelira combines a bounded original actor exercise, facial-performance cues, neutral-return safeguards and local causal review. [Competitor evidence](COMPETITOR_ANALYSIS.md).

I chose a prewritten scene instead of an AI chatbot so response content and signal provenance can be inspected and tested. The MVP does not need an external model, payment service or cloud database. Captions are always available; partner voice playback is not implemented. The optional recording is silent, so this is a rehearsal exercise rather than a full audition delivery/export suite.

My remaining competitive risk is reliability: large dramatic motion, lighting and differences between faces may prevent the required classifier labels. Provisional thresholds are not validation. Before submitting, I must show genuine accepted cues on Windows, document any cue adaptation, and acknowledge practical limitations instead of making claims about emotion, talent or artistic quality.
