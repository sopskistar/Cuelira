# Scene beats

I use three authored beats; expression labels are exercise controls, not universal meanings of faces.

| Beat          | Fictional action                      | Requested observation                                       | Meaningful partner change                                           |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Discovery     | Alex sees the concealment clearly     | Stable surprise label                                       | Rowan stops deflecting and admits the concealment                   |
| Confrontation | Alex denies Rowan's claim to decide   | Stable angry label                                          | Rowan accepts responsibility and reveals the fear behind the action |
| Decision      | Alex takes control of the next action | Stable neutral after a new deliberate non-neutral departure | Rowan gives back the phone; Alex gets the final line                |

I chose readable, repeatable cues for an exercise. An actor's subtle choices can exceed these labels; the software does not grade them. When recognition fails, manual continuation preserves the scene and marks the transition accurately. No live cue adaptation has yet been validated. A future adaptation must be based on actual supported scores, keep the story coherent, and be recorded in the discovery log.
