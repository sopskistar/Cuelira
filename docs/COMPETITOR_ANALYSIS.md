# Competitor assessment

I reviewed public submission descriptions and product websites on 19 November 2026. I did not reproduce competitors' hardware or certify their claims. Search coverage is incomplete; I do not claim Cuelira is the first facially responsive story or the only rehearsal product.

My partner answers dramatic facial cues rather than pacing a presentation.                         |

The current challenge tag also includes speaking-practice and character-animation submissions. I sampled the [tag](https://dev.to/t/presagechallenge) and older pages rather than treating it as an exhaustive competitor census.

## What I deliberately changed

I keep Rowan's answer visibly connected to its accepted observation, show the complete script before capture, require a neutral return within the current beat, and retain a take report. I explicitly record when the scene advances manually. Optional video, local export and deletion complete the actor's task. My final claim depends on authentic Windows proof, which is still outstanding.
