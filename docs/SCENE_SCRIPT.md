# The Last Message

I wrote this original short rehearsal exercise for Cuelira. Alex is the actor; Rowan is the scripted partner. There is no generated dialogue or psychological interpretation.

**Setting:** the corridor outside a nearly empty rehearsal room, late evening. Rowan has Alex's phone. An opportunity-changing message arrived days ago; Rowan hid it because Alex might leave.

**Alex's objective:** recover the phone and the right to choose, without letting Rowan turn the conversation into a request for reassurance. Estimated spoken material and pauses: 45–75 seconds, depending on pace. The engine does not force a 75-second cutoff.

## 1. The discovery

**Rowan:** I was going to give it to you. Just… not tonight.

**Alex:** That message was sent on Monday. You watched me wait all week. You knew what it meant to me.

_Perform the deliberate surprise cue and hold it briefly._

**Rowan's response:** I knew you would leave if you read it. I thought I could buy us one more day.

## 2. The confrontation

**Rowan:** One more day. That is all I wanted.

**Alex:** You didn’t buy a day. You took my choice. Look at me and say it was yours to take.

_Perform the deliberate angry / confrontational cue and hold it briefly._

**Rowan's response:** It wasn’t. I was afraid of being the person you left behind.

## 3. The decision

**Rowan:** Tell me what I can do now.

**Alex:** Give me the phone. No explanation. No promise. This time, I decide what happens next.

_After this beat starts, first hold a non-neutral expression; then deliberately release toward neutral and hold the return._

**Rowan's response:** Here. The message is still there. I haven’t touched it.

**Alex's closing line:** Then let me read it.

The complete script is expandable during preparation. During the take, both spoken parts stay captioned, and the previous partner reaction remains visible for continuity. Cuelira does not listen for spoken words or know when a line has been spoken; I pace the performance and the cue. There is no partner voice playback in this build.
