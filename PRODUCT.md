# Cuelira

<!-- impeccable:product-schema:1 -->

## Purpose

I am building one complete solo self-tape rehearsal: a scripted scene partner responds to accepted facial-performance cues from Presage SmartSpectra, followed by an observable take review.

## Platform

web

The UI runs inside a secure Windows Electron desktop application. Browser preview is for interface development only.

## Audience

Aspiring actors preparing a short audition alone, including emerging Nollywood performers. They need readable dialogue, minimal distractions and honest recovery when sensing fails.

## Core flow

Welcome → separate camera and optional video-recording consent → scene preparation → three live facial cues → review → local export or deletion.

## Stack

Electron, TypeScript, React, SmartSpectra 3.5.0, local application-data files. No paid AI or voice service.

## Principles

The name and tagline are final. Sponsor observations cause scene reactions. Manual actions remain manual. Observation labels never establish internal emotion or acting quality. Camera and private recordings remain under explicit user control.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard controls, captions always visible, reduced motion, a 960×680 desktop minimum, readable contrast, no reliance on color alone.

## Source and assumptions

Product decisions are supplied in my master build brief. I authorized autonomous implementation. Typography and visual direction remain implementation decisions; camera reliability and expression thresholds require my live acceptance.
